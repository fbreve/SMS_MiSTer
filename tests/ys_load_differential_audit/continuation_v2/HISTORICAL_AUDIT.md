Other expanded v19 facilities absent from master include latched H-counter history, NMI/peripheral protocol histories, mapper detector/Evolution pending histories, and later FM/audio handoff history. They are not bundled into the CPU candidate. The FM integration's optimized100-word transport remains entirely untouched; the user confirms the defect also exists before that PR.

The VDP control-port critical fields do **not** distinguish the historical implementation by a newly saved pending increment: broad v19 still restores xram_cpu_A/address_ff/read latch/read flag and clears xram_cpu_A_incr, data_write, and primes CPU edge detectors from live bus. No evidence supports adding an increment bit as the first fix. VRAM restoration may be correct while later guest writes use corrupted CPU/RAM state.

Existing evidence reused, without rerunning historical campaigns:

- `tests/savestate_g01_cpu_restart_fix/simulation_results.json`: 72 full restart groups, 18 known-bit traces, 17 accepted sweeps; separately recorded NMI triplets and repeated LOAD evidence. Those are historical validations, not current master end-to-end results.
- `tests/savestate_g06_vdp_sprites_fix/review.md`: complete 650-bit sprite diagnostic restoration still produced status reads at x28,62,... versus uninterrupted x26,60,...; the first two pixel enables were spent in CPU replay. Thus larger video snapshots alone did not fix release coherence. This is direct evidence for the independent timing portion of 58583189.
- `tests/savestate_g05_background_fix/baseline_results.json` and `sufficiency_pre_edit.json`: equal whole 98,304-byte v14 images, SHA2560ea3e9a697cdb342afbf9ffe0777413913b1e08c8fecb31f17a2382fa0a3baa4, with a mode-history-driven visible difference. This proves a general background-history defect, not Ys causality.

The history split is essential: CPU context, CPU/VDP temporal alignment, NMI sampling, and renderer history are independently meaningful mechanisms. One historical commit may package multiple mechanisms; its whole architecture is not the minimum causal unit.
