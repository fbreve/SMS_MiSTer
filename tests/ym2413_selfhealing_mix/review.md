## Independence and coverage

Candidate and unmodified committed reference are compiled into separate RTL
libraries. After deliberate candidate-only stale-state preparation and poison,
both receive the same restored image and identical subsequent input stimuli.
The reference is also restored, with all 104 fields checked against the
independently observed pre-SAVE image before unfreezing.

The architectural observations are independently packed from live registers,
process variables and RAMs, rather than generated by the SAVE read path. A second
ModelSim Tcl oracle reads the actual hierarchy and verifies all 104 observations
at 28 checkpoints. The phase-70 ModelSim and GHDL pilot traces match exactly:
4,292 rows, normalized SHA-256
`1399c6d4df5163898db34c815d5078c61882bd1f768d8cbd7c37adb9f77b4187`.

The long sweep uses GHDL 6.0.0; source and tool digests are in
`acc_results.json`. The portable archive SHA-256 is
`c1562b2a4767e34d33159b536b5e36a8a38ebdb6e7672aa9969a4f4ac2cadffe`. Launch scripts use Windows Low priority.
The stopped ModelSim performance campaigns are preserved as diagnostics and do not
count as acceptance passes. Native logs preserve explicit assertions, timeout and
exit status. `collect_acc.js` separately scans every CSV row and measured frame
boundary; it does not merely accept the simulator's final PASS message.

All 104 fields are compared continuously, allowing only the poisoned accumulator
and its bounded mixer-output consequence before convergence. Word-101's other
fields, feedback, phase, envelope RAM/state/lastkey, controller pipelines, custom
voices, rhythm/noise, and output memories must stay equal immediately. Additional
factory voice RAM and delayed controller counters are also checked. After
convergence every field and both raw/clamped output samples must remain equal.

Cases cover custom instruments, nine keyed channels, key-off/retrigger events,
rhythm/percussion flag changes, sustained execution and envelope transitions.
All four saved envelope pipeline encodings occur: Finish=0, Attack=1, Decay=2
and Release=3.

Poisons include signed maximum +8191, minimum -8192, an explicitly checked
opposite-sign extreme, zero, -1, alternating patterns, deterministic varied values
and negated saved sums. Accidental equality is adjusted. Every LOAD asserts that
the poison differs from the saved sum and survives restoration. The test-only
injection hook is disabled immediately after restore word 0, before word 101,
and remains disabled during resumed execution. A separate negative-control library
reinstating the assignment fails with `poison did not survive omitted restore`.

## Why the error cannot enter feedback in this design

The mixer's accumulator is read by its own add/subtract operation, snapshot
capture, and the transfer to `mixout`. It does not drive feedback, envelope,
phase, instrument RAM or controller state. At stage 2 / slot 17, the existing
operation transfers the partial sum to the output and clears `mix`. The poison
offset is constant modulo 14 bits until that reset. The following complete
72-enable cycle starts from equal zero accumulators and equal synthesis state,
so its output is equal. Continuous persistent-state assertions and the extended
follow-up experimentally check that isolation for every tested phase and poison.

This omission can therefore produce an incorrect partial sum on the audio output
for one 72-enable interval; it is not an immediately silent or bit-exact restore.

## Remaining limits and next experiment

These are RTL simulation results for the specified stimuli, not exhaustive formal
proof or MiSTer hardware validation. Downstream audio filtering/DAC state and the
audibility of the bounded transient were not measured. No FPGA resource saving is
claimed: the normal accumulator and output registers remain, and capture retains
all words. Synthesis is required to determine whether removing a restore mux has
any useful physical effect.

Recommend a **separate combined mixout + accumulator experiment**, based on the
same exact committed full-state reference. Independently poison both registers,
repeat the complete phase sweep and frame-based follow-up, and preserve this
accumulator-only worktree plus the validated mixout-only worktree as controls.
The combination requires authorization and has not been implemented. Quartus and
physical validation remain separate approval steps.
